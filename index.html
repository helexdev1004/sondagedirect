FAQ

Questions and answers

Everything you need to know before you start. Still stuck? Our support team is a message away.

Does it really work?

Yes. Take short surveys from trusted partners and earn points for every survey you complete successfully. Exchange those points for gift cards and the other rewards shown on this page.

What do I need to do to start earning rewards?

Create a free account, complete your profile, and start answering the surveys available to you. The more you take part, the faster your points add up.

Where will I receive survey invitations?

Invitations arrive by email — do check your junk or spam folder in case ours lands there. Surveys are also available here on the SondageDirect website.

How often will I be invited to take surveys?

We share surveys as often as we can, and as soon as we have one for you we send the link straight over. Check your inbox daily, and your junk or spam folder too in case our email ends up there.

What are the surveys about?

Topics vary widely — motoring, travel and leisure, food, drink and plenty more.

What kinds of rewards can I get?

Depending on how many points you have collected, you can exchange them for a range of rewards. Among them you will find PayPal, Virtual Visa, and vouchers and gift cards for brands such as Adidas, Google Play, IKEA and H&M, among many others.

How long does it take for my reward to arrive?

Once you have exchanged your points for a reward, we need a few days to check your request manually and send the reward to you by email.
